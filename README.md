# The Climb &amp; the Loop · 攀行與迴旋 · 登りと渦 · 오름과 소용돌이

**An animated oil diptych on how we think — artwork, words &amp; code by CKH.**

Two panels, one mind:

- **Panel I · The Process** — dawn. Morning blue melting to gold, a rising sun, and a small
  figure slowly climbing a path of stones: every stone a marked failure, every stone a step.
  Birds flap toward the light. *Think positive → even in falling, you are still walking —
  every failure a stone, every stone a step of becoming successful.*
- **Panel II · The Loop** — night. A bowed figure kneels on the cracked slab of *the mistake*,
  encircled by a slowly breathing vortex of *remember → blame self → blame others*, while a
  small spark labelled *success* hangs far away, its trail fading before it arrives.
  *Think negative → the loop, and success drifts farther, ever farther away.*

**Live:** <https://kiuckhuang.github.io/thinking-in-art/>

## Features

- **Single self-contained HTML file** — no frameworks, no external assets, works offline over `file://`
- Everything painted procedurally with a custom **oil-brush engine** (tapered impasto strokes,
  scumbled skies, grain, varnish, canvas weave)
- **Living details:** the climber ascends stone by stone, birds flap toward the sun, the vortex
  of blame breathes, warm motes rise / cold ash falls
- **Five languages** — English · 繁體中文（香港）· 简体中文 · 日本語 · 한국어 — including the
  words painted *inside* the canvases; auto-detected, switchable, remembered
- Honors `prefers-reduced-motion`; a **Motion pill** can override it
- Invisible two-layer provenance watermark (*by CKH*) + attribution metadata — please don't strip it
