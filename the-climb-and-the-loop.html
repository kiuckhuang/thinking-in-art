A diptych in oil — two studies of one mind

The Climb & the Loop

one mind, two weathers — the same brush, two directions of the heart

Panel I · The Process

Think positive even in falling, you are still walking — every failure a stone, every stone a step of becoming.

as first sketched — “even failed, take it as a process of being successful.”

Panel II · The Loop

Think negative the mistake kept forever, blame circling self and others, success drifting farther, ever farther away.

as first sketched — “forever remember the mistake and blaming self or others → more far away from being successful.”

as every brief commands — keep the palette · keep the brushwork · keep the mood
